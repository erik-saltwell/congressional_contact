# Intent: Research multi-contact import on iOS and Android

## Outcome

Find an answer to this question, with enough supporting evidence that the project can make a decision:

> Does single-click import of several contacts at once (for example one multi-contact vCard file) work reliably on both iOS and Android, and what is the best fallback if it does not?

## Background

This is one of three open questions recorded in [.agent_context.md](../../.agent_context.md) for the Congressional Contact project: a static web page, ideally one file, with no backend, accounts, or API keys. It lets a phone user save their two senators and their House representative as full-detail contacts. The goal is for the contacts to end up saved in the phone's contacts app; a plain download is the acceptable fallback.

## Scope

Answering the question above. Choosing the final design and building it are separate work.

## Acceptance

A written answer to the question that names its sources, states the practical options and their tradeoffs against the project's constraints, and notes anything that stayed uncertain.
